Course overview

Dialogue and Quest Systems in Godot

Build NPC conversations, track quest progress, and stage story scenes.

This course is for developers who know how to work with scenes and scripts in Godot and want to add story systems to their RPG.

What you’ll learn

  1. Structure dialogue data and build the first NPC interaction.
  2. Track quest objectives and give players feedback as they progress.
  3. Coordinate cameras, characters, and dialogue in story scenes.
